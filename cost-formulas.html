Cost data and formulas

The workbook is also source evidence

Excel files, worksheets, schedules, formulas, and estimate workbooks show how MARPA already thinks about quantities, categories, pricing, margin, and project cost. We preserve that logic instead of flattening it into anonymous rows.

What we extract from workbooks

  • sheet names and workbook structure
  • headers, line items, totals, subtotals
  • formulas and cell dependencies
  • quantity units and measurement basis
  • labor, material, equipment, markup, margin
  • project/category/cost-code naming patterns

Example formula logic

extended_cost = quantity * unit_rate subtotal = material_cost + labor_cost + equipment_cost estimated_total = subtotal * (1 + markup_rate) risk_range = estimated_total ± contingency
