Why Graftkit exists

MARPA's project knowledge lives across drawings, schedules, layers, classes, worksheets, PDFs, IFC, images, plant records, resource assets, and financial history. Those sources are valuable, but they do not automatically explain each other.

Graftkit creates the governed system that connects them. It starts from real design evidence, keeps source context attached, and turns that evidence into useful review surfaces, technical BIM/GIS workflows, plant and material asset control, and financial intelligence.

The goal is not another dashboard. The goal is a reliable project intelligence foundation where the team can explain where an answer came from and why it should be trusted.

Process: design state to usable intelligence

The system preserves the design state, extracts every useful representation, builds evidence packages, normalizes project concepts, serves review surfaces, and only then writes approved information forward.

1. Design stateVectorworks owns spatial design context, layers, classes, objects, sheets, symbols, and plant records.
2. HarvestGather IFC, DWG/DXF, PDFs, worksheets, schedules, images, resources, and financial files.
3. EvidenceStore source references, hashes, extraction receipts, pages, rows, layers, and object context.
4. NormalizeCreate stable MARPA identities, quantities, classifications, assets, costs, and IFC candidates.
5. ReviewUse BIM/GIS, 3D, Marimo, asset, finance, and documentation surfaces for human review.
6. Write forwardPublish reports, snapshots, validation packages, and future approved MCP/IFC writeback.

Six coordinated lanes

Graftkit is one governed platform with six lanes. A lane owns a concern, not the whole truth. The lanes share manifests, source registries, contracts, receipts, and validation rules.

Lane 1 · 3D Rendering / CinematicGeoglify, Soil Studio, Three.js/R3F, and rendering bridge pieces merge into one governed client-facing 3D application.
Lane 2 · BIM / Technical OperationsGeoLibre, OpenAEC, ifc-lite, validation, BCF, schedule, and cost workflows support working BIM/GIS operations.
Lane 3 · Vectorworks BridgeVectorworks SDK, scripting, MCP, document context, export control, and future writeback protect the design authority.
Lane 4 · Documentation / Knowledge BaseStakeholder proof, specs, architecture records, runbooks, handoffs, verification reports, and knowledge graphs.
Lane 5 · Asset Creation / Plant ManagementPlant and hardscape assets, reference media, GLBs, profiles, deterministic exports, and manifest-based serving.
Lane 6 · Financial IntelligenceCost evidence, budgets, CSI classification, DuckDB snapshots, Marimo models, and future predictive estimating.

Truth model and governance

The architecture keeps design truth, evidence truth, analytical truth, work truth, and source-code truth separate. That separation prevents one tool from quietly becoming responsible for everything.

VectorworksDesign and spatial truth: geometry, layers, classes, sheets, symbols, plant records, and live project context.
PixeltableMultimodal evidence and enrichment truth: source artifacts, extracted content, embeddings, receipts, and lineage.
DuckDB / DuckLakeAnalytical serving and snapshot truth: reports, marts, model-ready data, and reproducible financial baselines.
Marimo / AnyWidgetReview and modeling surface: scenario controls, approvals, financial review, and data-quality inspection.
GitHubRepository and project operations truth: commits, branches, pull requests, issues, checks, templates, and board state.
opensrcLocal source intelligence truth: cached source, docs, APIs, licenses, and adoption decisions before implementation.
Design authority
Vectorworks remains the source for the design state.
Evidence authority
Pixeltable stores source artifacts, extraction outputs, enrichments, and receipts.
Serving authority
DuckDB/DuckLake publish stable analytical snapshots and model-ready tables.
Review surfaces
Marimo, BIM/GIS tools, 3D scenes, reports, and stakeholder pages make the evidence usable.
Work authority
GitHub, CI, source registries, manifests, rules, and receipts control implementation work.
