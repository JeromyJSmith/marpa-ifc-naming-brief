## Short Version

Graftkit turns MARPA's design files, project evidence, plant assets, construction information, and financial history into one governed operating system.

The work starts from the real design environment, especially Vectorworks. It extracts evidence from drawings, schedules, IFC, worksheets, PDFs, images, asset libraries, and financial records. That evidence is organized into traceable data layers, reviewed through human-readable surfaces, and used to support BIM/GIS operations, 3D presentations, plant management, financial intelligence, and future predictive estimating.

The goal is not another dashboard. The goal is a reliable project intelligence platform where every number, object, asset, and decision can be traced back to its source.

## Core Message For Stakeholders

MARPA already has valuable project knowledge spread across drawings, files, notebooks, spreadsheets, schedules, images, resources, and operator experience. Graftkit creates the governed system that connects those pieces without flattening them into one fragile tool.

It gives the team:

- cleaner project evidence;
- better takeoff and cost foundations;
- clearer BIM and IFC structure;
- stronger client-facing 3D and proof surfaces;
- traceable plant and material assets;
- financial models grounded in real operating data;
- agent-assisted workflows with receipts and review gates.
